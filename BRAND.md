# Finqit.ai brand foundation

## Positioning
Finqit.ai is the trusted operating layer for apartment living: discover and secure a new home, then manage the community you live in.

## Brand idea
**Move first. Live better.**

Finqit should feel fast enough for a competitive property market and trustworthy enough for reservations, community finances, documents and voting.

## Identity
- Product name: **Finqit.ai**
- Short name: **Finqit**
- Primary mark: rounded blue home tile with a doorway and AI spark
- Wordmark: dark navy `Finqit` with blue `.ai`
- Avoid using the former `Thinkit.ai` name in any product or marketing asset.

## Product design principles
1. Trust before cleverness — reservation economics, queue rules and community actions must always be explicit.
2. iOS-like tactility — generous rounded corners, soft elevation, restrained motion and large tap targets.
3. Airbnb-level clarity — imagery and task completion dominate over admin-heavy chrome.
4. Blue means action and confidence — reserve stronger blue for primary actions, active states and verified intelligence.
5. Mobile-first architecture — every desktop interaction must have a clear mobile equivalent suitable for a future native shell.
